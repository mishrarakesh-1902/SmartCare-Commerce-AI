Ajara Shopper - Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Product Policy

1. Reporting Timeline:
If you receive an item that is damaged, broken, has manufacturing defects, or differs from what you ordered, please report it within 48 hours of delivery via the AI Support Desk.

2. Proof of Damage:
Customers are requested to describe the defect and, if possible, keep the outer courier packaging and product photos ready. An unboxing video is recommended for high-value electronics and apparel.

3. Resolution Options:
- Instant Free Replacement: A brand new replacement item is dispatched within 24 hours at zero additional cost.
- 100% Full Refund: If the product is out of stock or the customer prefers not to receive a replacement, a full refund including shipping charges is credited immediately.

4. Doorstep Reverse Pickup:
Our courier partner will inspect and collect the damaged product from your doorstep. No return shipping fee is charged for defective or damaged items.

5. Priority Handling:
Any report of damaged or defective items is automatically tagged as HIGH PRIORITY by our AI Support Agent to guarantee response and action within minutes.
