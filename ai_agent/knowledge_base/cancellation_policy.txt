Ajara Shopper - Order Cancellation Policy

1. Cancellation Before Dispatch:
Customers can cancel any order free of charge at any time before it has been dispatched from our warehouse. The cancellation is instant and can be done via the AI Support Desk or 'Your Order' page.

2. Refund for Cancelled Orders:
For prepaid orders cancelled before dispatch:
- 100% full refund is issued instantly.
- The amount is reflected in your bank/card within 24 to 48 hours.

3. Cancellation After Dispatch:
Once an order has been handed over to the courier partner, it cannot be digitally cancelled in transit. In such cases:
- The customer may simply refuse to accept delivery when the courier arrives at their doorstep.
- Once the package is marked 'Return to Origin' (RTO), the full refund will be processed immediately.

4. Partial Cancellation:
For orders containing multiple items, individual items can be cancelled before dispatch without cancelling the entire order.
