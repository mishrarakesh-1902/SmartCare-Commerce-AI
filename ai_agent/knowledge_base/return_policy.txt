Ajara Shopper - Return & Exchange Policy

1. Return Window:
Customers can initiate a return or exchange request within 7 calendar days from the date of delivery. After 7 days, returns cannot be processed through the automated system and require special manager approval.

2. Product Condition:
Items must be in unused, unwashed condition with original tags, brand packaging, and barcode stickers intact. Innerwear, customized products, and items marked 'Final Sale' are non-returnable.

3. Free Exchanges:
Size and color exchanges are 100% free with doorstep pickup and simultaneous replacement delivery.

4. Return Shipping Fee:
- If an item is defective, damaged, or the wrong item was sent, return pickup is completely FREE.
- For returns due to change of mind or personal preference, a nominal pickup fee of ₹50 is deducted from the refund.

5. Pickup Process:
Once a return is approved, our courier partner will pick up the package within 24 to 48 hours. A digital pickup receipt (RMA code) will be sent via SMS and Email.
